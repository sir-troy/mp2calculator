Common questions

Short answers for first-time savers.

The basics

What is MP2, in simple words?

MP2 (Modified Pag-IBIG 2) is a voluntary savings program of Pag-IBIG Fund. You put money in, leave it for 5 years, and it earns extra money every year. It's separate from the regular Pag-IBIG contribution that's deducted from your salary.

What is a "dividend"?

It's the extra money MP2 adds to your savings every year. Think of it like interest in a bank, but usually much higher. At 7.12%, ₱10,000 saved for a full year earns about ₱712.

How is MP2 different from my regular Pag-IBIG savings?

Your regular Pag-IBIG savings (also called Pag-IBIG I or P1) is required and usually deducted from your salary.

MP2 is optional, you choose how much and when to save, it matures in 5 years, and its dividend rate is set higher than the regular savings rate.

Who can open an MP2 account?
  • Active Pag-IBIG members, regardless of income
  • Former members who are pensioners or have other monthly income, if they had at least 24 monthly savings before retiring
  • Former natural-born Filipinos who got their citizenship back under R.A. 9225
Is my money safe in MP2?

MP2 is run by Pag-IBIG Fund, a government agency, and its savings are guaranteed by the national government. Like any savings decision, read the official terms before putting in money.

Do I pay tax on MP2 dividends?

No. MP2 dividends are tax-free, so the amount you see in this calculator is not reduced by tax.

Saving

What's the minimum and maximum I can save?

The minimum is ₱500 per deposit. There's no set maximum. For big amounts: payments above ₱100,000 need proof of income or source of funds, and one-time payments above ₱500,000 must be paid by personal or manager's check.

Do I have to save every single month?

No. You can skip months, change the amount anytime, or deposit just once. Use the "My own amounts" tab above to see what happens when your deposits are different each month.

Is it better to put in one big amount or save monthly?

Money that goes in earlier earns dividends longer, so a big deposit at the start usually earns more than the same amount spread over 5 years. But saving monthly is easier for most people. Try both in the calculator and compare.

How do I open an account and pay?

You can enroll online through Virtual Pag-IBIG on the Pag-IBIG Fund website, or at any Pag-IBIG branch. After you get your MP2 account number, you can pay through Virtual Pag-IBIG, accredited payment partners, or at a branch. Always check the official list of payment channels.

Can I have more than one MP2 account?

Yes, many members open several MP2 accounts, for example one for each goal or one started each year. Each account has its own 5-year term.

Dividends

Is the dividend rate guaranteed every year?

No. Pag-IBIG Fund announces a new rate every year, usually around February or March, based on how much it earned. If you want a safer estimate, tap the "Cautious" button.

Should I keep the dividend in, or get it every year?

Keep it in if you don't need the money yet. Your dividends also earn dividends, so you end up with more.

Get it every year if you want a yearly "allowance" from your savings. Your final total is smaller, but you receive some money along the way.

Why is my result slightly different from Pag-IBIG's?

This calculator assumes you deposit at the start of each month. Pag-IBIG computes using your actual posting dates, so real amounts will be a little different. Use this as a guide, not an exact figure.

Withdrawing

Can I withdraw before 5 years?

Yes, but only for certain reasons, and you get your full dividends only for these reasons:

  • Total disability or insanity
  • Death of the member or an immediate family member
  • Permanently leaving the country
  • Critical illness of the member or an immediate family member
  • Other reasons approved by Pag-IBIG's Board

For any other reason, you only get 50% of the dividends earned.

What happens when my 5 years are up?

You can claim your savings and dividends. If you want to keep saving, open a new MP2 account. If you don't claim it, your money stops earning MP2 dividends. For the next 2 years it earns only the lower regular Pag-IBIG rate.
