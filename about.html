About MP2 Calculator

Estimate how your Pag-IBIG MP2 savings could grow in 5 years.

What it does

MP2 Calculator is an unofficial, easy-to-read calculator for the Pag-IBIG MP2 (Modified Pag-IBIG II) savings program. Enter a monthly amount — or your own amount for each month — and it shows how much you could have after the 5-year term, year by year, using past dividend rates. It works in English and Tagalog.

How to use it

  1. Choose “Same amount every month” or “My own amounts”.
  2. Type your monthly savings and, if you like, a one-time deposit.
  3. Pick whether dividends are kept in (compounded) or paid out every year.
  4. Read the year-by-year chart and table, or copy your results to share.

Why I made this

These are tools I actually need in my day-to-day work and life. Instead of uploading my stuff to a stranger's server every time, I built small versions that run entirely in the browser. Nothing to upload, nothing to retain.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the official Pag-IBIG calculator?

No. This site is not associated with, endorsed by, or operated by Pag-IBIG Fund (HDMF). For official information, visit pagibigfund.gov.ph.

How accurate are the results?

They are estimates based on past dividend rates. Future rates are not guaranteed, so your real payout can be higher or lower.

Is what I type saved?

No. All the math runs in your browser and nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere.

Can I use it in Tagalog?

Yes — switch between English and Tagalog at the top of the page.

Your privacy, in short

MP2 Calculator does its work in your browser. The calculator does not save anything you type. Close the tab and it's gone. Read the full Privacy Policy.

MP2 Calculator gives estimates only and is not financial advice. It is not affiliated with Pag-IBIG Fund (HDMF). Dividend rates change every year and past rates do not guarantee future results. Check official sources before making financial decisions.
